# Public claims policy

**Can state:** selected subgraph savings in R5A0; R47 short fresh-data results relative to Native+D9; R57 isolated EM4 vs native and checkpointing; real D9 storage compression vs FP32; and R60/R62 measured output-loss effects with clear attribution to a public optimization family.

**Cannot state without new experiments:** "16 GB behaves like 32 GB"; that SHV supports arbitrary LLMs; training and inference both proven; production-ready CUDA integration; speedup over standard PyTorch; no training-quality degradation in long training; general superiority over FlashAttention/Liger/Cut Cross-Entropy/checkpointing/8-bit optimizers; a finished HOT/COLD runtime; publicly licensed/downloadable source before actual publication.

Different accuracy measures are not interchangeable: exact forward output, cosine of selected gradients, short validation-loss deviation, and end-of-training quality test different properties. For all performance metrics record both the baseline and the measured workload scope.
